    About

    I'm a global brand strategist, art director and documentary & film producer focused on growth and emerging markets. The strategy, the creative and the camera come from the same place.

    I partner with global brands, scaleups and impact-driven ventures to build stories that convert and hold up on every platform. That has meant product launches in India, rebrands in Argentina and a stint as interim CMO for an agritech company in Indonesia.

    Lately I've been producing multi-country documentary series and impact case studies for development finance, coordinating crews remotely or flying in to produce on the ground, and shaping the story into a business case investors and customers act on.

    What you'll find here is a mix of campaigns, creative direction and brand systems built to scale. I'm based in Barcelona and work remotely with teams around the world.
