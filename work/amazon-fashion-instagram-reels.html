Amazon Fashion

Instagram Reels

Amazon Fashion's first content strategy campaign exclusively for Instagram.

  • Creative fieldsArt Direction, Fashion, Film

Amazon India's first content strategy campaign, crafted exclusively for Instagram Reels, achieved remarkable success in its pilot phase. Partnering with POPxo, India's largest online community for millennial women, the campaign aimed to position Amazon Fashion India as the go-to destination for budget-conscious shoppers.

Measurable results

This campaign delivered a strong ROI by amplifying brand visibility and achieving 59% engagement across Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube. For Amazon Fashion, the campaign directly impacted the private labels, driving significant traffic to product pages and achieving an impressive 48% conversion rate.
