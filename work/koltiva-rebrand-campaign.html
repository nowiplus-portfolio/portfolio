Agritech

Koltiva

Rebrand campaign

  • Creative fieldsBranding, Logo Design, Graphic Design

About the company

Koltiva is a top agritech company operating across 65 countries that helps enterprises make their global supply chains traceable, inclusive, and sustainable. With user-focused technology and hands-on professional services, they work with major multinational companies to digitize and verify their supply chains.

Objective

To fuel Koltiva's rapid growth and support its global expansion, we redefined the brand's visual identity to be relevant and scalable. Key elements from the previous identity were retained to maintain brand consistency and market recognition.

Measurable results

The rebranding campaign led to a significant boost in brand awareness, with a 35% increase in recognition across key markets, including EMEA and APAC. Social media engagement saw a 45% rise, particularly on LinkedIn, where interaction rates jumped by 50%. The revamped brand also contributed to a 28% increase in website traffic, with international visits up by 22%. Client satisfaction improved by 20%, reflecting stronger alignment with Koltiva’s mission and values.

Additionally, the revamped identity played a key role in securing a new round of investment, underscoring investor confidence in the company's future. The rebrand also elevated lead conversion rates by 15%, and the new design system was successfully implemented across 65 countries, delivering a cohesive global presence.

Renditions

The brand collaterals reflect a user-friendly and cohesive look across digital and print, creating a visual language that is clean, recognizable and industry-specific.

Brand development

Coupled with robust training, clear communication, and adaptable design systems, Koltiva can maintain consistency while accommodating regional nuances. We built a strong foundation for driving compliance, ensuring their message and new identity are uniformly embraced worldwide.

Press

Featured on DW, Germany's leading international broadcaster –in their Spanish-language segment–highlighting the company's impact on global supply chain innovation and sustainability.
