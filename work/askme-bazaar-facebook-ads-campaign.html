AskMe Bazaar – Live Extra Ad Campaign

This project was designed to position the brand as a leading online marketplace by driving consumer awareness, engagement, and conversions through a high-impact online campaign. The objective was to establish AskMe Bazaar as the go-to platform for seamless online shopping, targeting value-conscious consumers in India's growing e-commerce sector.

Through a combination of compelling storytelling, influencer collaborations, and strategic media placements, the campaign achieved a 35% increase in website traffic, a 20% growth in daily transactions, and a significant uplift in brand recall within key demographics. By aligning with industry trends and consumer behaviors, the campaign reinforced AskMe Bazaar’s credibility in a competitive market, translating visibility into tangible business growth. Sadly, the platform shut operations in 2016.
