Caviahue Sustentable – The Rebranding Campaign

The new identity was developed to elevate the brand’s market positioning and strengthen its identity as a premium ski resort in Neuquén, Patagonia Argentina. The objective was to shift Caviahue’s corporate visual identity into a more sustainable brand, implementing a communication strategy designed to appeal to an eco-conscious audience.

The results

Through a refined logo, consistent messaging, and a cohesive online presence, the campaign led to a 45% increase in brand engagement on social media and a 30% boost in e-commerce sales. This strategic rebranding reinforced Caviahue’s credibility in the competitive hospitality industry, aligning the brand with consumer demand for premium eco-ski destinations.
