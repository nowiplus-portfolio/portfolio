Love in a Box campaign

Love in a Box is an online campaign to encourage customers to celebrate Valentine's Day above and beyond romantic clichés. Amazon Gift Card suggests its easy gifting option for all your loved ones, no matter your relationship status.

  • Creative fieldsAnimation, Art Direction, Advertising

Love in a Box is a campaign that redefined Valentine’s Day by shifting the focus beyond traditional romantic clichés, inviting everyone to celebrate love in all its forms.

Designed for Amazon Gift Cards, a versatile and customer-friendly gifting solution used by millions year-round, this on-site campaign highlighted the message: “One gift card, endless options—no matter your relationship status.” By moving past the typical hearts-and-roses narrative, the campaign encouraged inclusivity and creative gifting, positioning Amazon Gift Cards as the perfect choice for any connection and occasion.

Measurable results

During the campaign, sales surged by 40% compared to the previous year’s Valentine’s Day period, with a sustained 25% year-over-year increase in the two weeks that followed. The campaign led to a +15% in the conversion rate. It attracted a broader audience, particularly singles, friends, and families, resulting in a +35% in first-time gift card purchasers. The average order value (AOV) also saw an 18% boost, as customers opted for higher denominations to give more versatile gifts.

Additionally, the campaign sparked over 500,000 social media interactions, mainly user-generated content (UGC) sharing creative ways to gift cards.
