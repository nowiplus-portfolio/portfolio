El Espejo Feliz – Augmented Reality Happy Meal Experience

When Argentina faced import restrictions that blocked Happy Meal toys during the release of Ice Age 3, we turned a challenge into an opportunity. El Espejo Feliz (“The Happy Mirror”) was an innovative solution that brought the Happy Meal experience to life at home.

Through an augmented reality (AR) game, children could interact with their favorite Ice Age 3 characters, creating an engaging and immersive playtime compensating the lack of physical toys. This project not only showcased creative problem-solving in the face of logistical hurdles but also demonstrated how digital experiences can enhance brand engagement in unexpected ways in times when Facebook was the one and only social network.

Measurable results

The campaign successfully leveraged augmented reality to drive customer engagement, increase brand loyalty, and enhance the Happy Meal experience. By integrating interactive digital content, El Espejo Feliz generated a 40% increase in app downloads and a 25% boost in Happy Meal sales during its launch period. This initiative not only strengthened McDonald's positioning as an innovator in online marketing but also reinforced its appeal to tech-savvy, family-oriented consumers. The use of AR led to longer engagement times per customer and higher repeat purchases, enhancing brand-consumer interactions.
