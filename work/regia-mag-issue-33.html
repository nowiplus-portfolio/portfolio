For The New Rising Issue, REGIA invited me to collaborate featuring photos of my early days in India.

Issue No. 33
–
The New Rising

Nowi is an Argentinian living in Bangalore, where she works as an Art Director at Amazon. In this guest editorial, we invited her to share her first impressions of the country that is now her home.

…

"India was never part of the plan. I arrived here for work—and out of sheer curiosity.
I had explored maps, street view, and countless videos, but none of them were enough to truly understand a country like this.

Every day brings something new: words, sounds, visuals, smells—situations that might seem extraordinary in the West. It’s a land of contrasts, where extremes blend effortlessly. The most captivating place I’ve ever visited—everything feels as if it belongs to another dimension.

When I decided to embark on this journey, my then-boyfriend gave me a guide to India, which conveniently stated: "Expect the unexpected." And so I did.

Coming from the other side of the world, I quickly realized just how different our realities are. The first thing that hits you is the inevitable culture shock—even something as simple as buying bed sheets can become an adventure!

I can’t stop taking photos to document my experience. I immerse myself in local food, clothing, music, films, and even pick up basic Hindi—anything that helps me decode the cultural richness of this ancient land.

You either love it or hate it. Or, quite possibly, both.
If you ask me whether I’d do this again, my answer would be: a thousand times over."

…
